What we do

Six connected sectors that make up an integrated food ecosystem, from the production line to the customer.

01

Food manufacturing

We develop and manufacture high-quality food products for the Saudi market, applying the best standards of industry and innovation.

Through Abdullah Alsubaie Food Production in Jeddah, we produce baked goods and pastries with organised manufacturing capacity and a focus on food safety and consistent quality.

Group company: Abdullah Alsubaie Food Production Co.
02

Restaurant & retail chains

We own and operate restaurant and retail chains in Saudi Arabia, Kuwait and Qatar with unified standards and exceptional quality.

Khaliat Khalid leads this sector with branches across the Kingdom and beyond, and Kbagi Algharbia joins it soon. Every branch runs to the same operating standards.

Group company: Khaliat KhalidKbagi Algharbia
03

Catering

We provide complete catering services for institutions and major events, to high standards and with refined taste.

We serve corporate, institutional and event orders with selections from our brands, planning quantities and delivery to suit each occasion.

Group company: Khaliat KhalidAbdullah Alsubaie Food Production Co.
04

Brands

We create and develop Saudi brands with a strong identity and a real place in the minds of Saudi and Gulf consumers.

Each brand gets its own identity and story, from the familiar blue of Khaliat Khalid to Kbagi Algharbia, inspired by the Hijazi rawshan.

Group company: Khaliat KhalidKbagi Algharbia
05

Quality & sustainability

We hold every stage of production and service to the highest quality standards, and work in ways that protect the environment and community.

Quality starts with how we choose suppliers and continues until the product reaches the customer. We work to reduce waste and choose more responsible packaging.

Group company: Abdullah Alsubaie Food Production Co.
06

Investment & development

We invest in promising opportunities and keep developing our business for sustainable growth, diversified income and innovation.

We look at new opportunities across the food sector, from launching new brands to partnerships and franchising, that add long-term value for the group and its partners.

Let's build something together

We welcome partners, investors and suppliers interested in joining our ecosystem. We reply to enquiries within 48 working hours.
