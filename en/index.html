About us

Building exceptional Saudi food enterprises

A Saudi company founded in 2020, dedicated to building and managing an integrated ecosystem in the food sector through a portfolio of specialised companies and brands. We aim to build a sustainable food enterprise that can grow and expand, and to create strong brands with lasting value, in line with the goals of Saudi Vision 2030.
